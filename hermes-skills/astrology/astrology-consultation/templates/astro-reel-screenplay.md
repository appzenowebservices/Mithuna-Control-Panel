# Template — Astrological Short-Reel Screenplay (vertical 9:16)

Reusable shape for Astrologics short-form video content ("THE SKY IS TALKING" series
style, SDS-83 2026-09-04). Copy + fill: keep the structure, swap the content per
episode. Delivery mechanics live in `board-ops` → `references/sds-83-capability-delivery.md`.

## Fixed structure (45-60 s)

1. **Cold open (0-3 s)** — black frame → starfield rush toward camera; hook overlay
   (e.g. "The sky has been trying to reach you."); heartbeat + low drone SFX.
2. **Hook (3-10 s)** — moon-phase timelapse / sunrise; VO stating the premise
   (e.g. "Every night, two thousand stars pass overhead… and each one is talking about
   you."); series-label overlay.
3. **Message (10-35 s)** — quick-cut montage on VO; ONE archetype line per sign group:
   fire (be seen) / earth (you already have enough — build) / air (the answer is a
   question you haven't asked) / water (feel it, then move).
4. **Turn (35-45 s)** — slow push-in on a mirror/window reflection with stars behind;
   the twist (e.g. "You're not the reader. You're the author."). Brand's recurring pivot.
5. **CTA (45-60 s)** — title card on starfield + follow line + cadence
   (e.g. "New moons, retrogrades, every myth — on time, every Monday.").

## CURRENT-EVENT SLOT (optional, ~8 s) — the ONLY factual slot

Insert between Hook and Message. VO: "This week: [EVENT NAME]. [one-line astrological
meaning]." + overlay "[EVENT NAME] — [DATE]".

**RULE: never invent the event, date, or meaning.** If the operator hasn't supplied the
real sky event, leave the slot as a fill-in and ask for it. Creative license never
extends to ephemeris facts.

## Production notes

- Ratio 9:16 (1080×1920); captions burned-in lower-third, below 85% height.
- VO ≈ 110 words ≈ 45 s; one take, intimate tone, slight reverb; music bed ~-18 dB under VO.
- Assets: starfield timelapse, moon-phase cycle, 4 archetype b-rolls, dark-window mirror
  shot, title card.
- Series reuse: keep hook + twist + CTA constant; rotate archetype lines / event slot weekly.
