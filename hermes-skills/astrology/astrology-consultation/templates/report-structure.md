# Consultation Report Template

Personalized written-reading structure for Astrologics consultations. Every
section is mandatory in a full natal reading; short-form readings (a single
life-theme deep dive) may compress sections 3-5 but must keep the opening,
the question section, the closing, and the disclaimer. Live sessions follow
the same arc verbally.

---

## 1. Opening

- Greet the client by name; restate the question they asked, in their words.
- State the chart basis: birth date, time, place, and the timezone used.
- One or two sentences on what this reading covers (and does not).

## 2. Core self — the big three

- Sun: sign, degree, house — the identity engine.
- Moon: sign, degree, house — the emotional engine.
- Ascendant: sign, degree — the manner, plus its ruler's placement.
- One or two sentences weaving the three together into a single portrait.

## 3. The question, answered

- The chart factors for the client's life area (house, ruler, planets
  there, key aspects) — named with actual placements.
- The pattern: what supports the direction, what complicates it, what
  timing or conditions matter.
- A direct, honest answer to the question as asked, framed as tendencies
  and conditions.

## 4. Supporting detail

- Aspects that shape the picture (major ones only, by name and orb).
- Anything distinctive: a stellium, a singleton, an intercepted house, a
  powerful applying aspect, retrograde emphasis.
- Keep this section tied to the question; it is support, not a chart dump.

## 5. Practical notes

- 2-4 concrete, chart-grounded suggestions (e.g. "with your Mars in Aries
  in the 6th, structure the launch as short sprints with visible wins").
- For forecast readings: dated transit windows with the actual degrees.

## 6. Closing

- A short, warm summary of the strongest takeaway — the one sentence the
  client should remember.
- Next steps (follow-up reading, subscription, testimonial ask per ops).

## 7. Standard disclaimer (always, verbatim)

> This reading is provided for reflection and entertainment purposes only.
> It is not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice. Decisions
> you make are your own responsibility — please consult qualified
> professionals for matters of health, law, or money. Birth data shared
> with Astrologics is treated as private and is used only to prepare your
> reading.

## PII rules

- Never include the client's birth data (date/time/place) in the report
  body beyond what the client needs — the chart basis line is enough.
- Client records (intake, chart parameters, review disposition) live on
  the client's board issue only.
- Sample/pilot readings must be built from explicit synthetic birth data,
  labeled PILOT, and carry the same disclaimer.
