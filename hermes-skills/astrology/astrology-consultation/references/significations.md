# Significations & Interpretation Reference

Supporting reference for the astrology-consultation skill. Use it at Procedure
steps 4-5: map the client's question to chart factors, then interpret those
factors in a narrative tied to the question. This is a working reference —
interpretation judgment stays with the consultant; the checklist enforces the
output bar.

## Question → chart-area mapping

| Question / life area | Primary factors | Supporting factors |
|---|---|---|
| Career, vocation, public standing | 10th house, Midheaven (MC), 6th house | Saturn, Sun, house-10 ruler, planets conjunct MC |
| Money, income, possessions | 2nd house, 8th house | Venus, Jupiter, Pluto, house-2 ruler |
| Relationships, partnership | 7th house (and 5th for romance) | Venus, Moon, Mars, house-7 ruler, synastry in relationship readings |
| Home, family, roots | 4th house, IC | Moon, house-4 ruler |
| Identity, self-expression, creative work | 1st, 5th houses | Sun, Ascendant, house-5 ruler |
| Communication, learning, ideas | 3rd house, 9th house | Mercury, Jupiter, house-3 ruler |
| Health, daily rhythm | 6th house, 1st house | Moon, Mars, house-6 ruler (health: never medical advice). If ONE planet rules both the 1st and 6th cusps, it is the double health significator — anchor the reading on it (SDS-56: Venus ruled both Asc 21 Taurus and 6th cusp 12 Libra) |
| Timing, "when", major life shifts | Transiting Saturn/Jupiter/Uranus/Pluto to natal factors (forecast services) | Solar return, progressed Moon for yearly work |
| "Should I take this risk?" | The house of the thing + the aspect pattern of its ruler | Saturn (caution), Jupiter (expansion), Mars (impulse) |

Method: identify the area, pull the primary factors, check the aspects
involving them (script output), and read the pattern as one coherent story —
not a list of unrelated facts.

## The big three

- **Sun** — core identity, vitality, what you are becoming. Sign = style of
  self-expression; house = arena where identity gets built.
- **Moon** — emotional nature, instincts, needs, memory, home. Sign = how
  feelings work; house = where security is sought.
- **Ascendant (rising)** — the mask and the manner, first impressions, the
  body's temperament. Its ruler shapes the life path.

Intercepted or cuspidal planets: interpret by house and sign; note intercepted
houses (two house cusps in the same sign) as areas that take longer to
activate.

## Planets (quick significations)

- **Sun** — identity, vitality, authority, the father (traditional).
- **Moon** — emotion, habit, caregiving, the mother (traditional), the public
  in a natal 10th context.
- **Mercury** — mind, speech, commerce, travel of the everyday kind.
- **Venus** — attraction, values, money (as resource), art, diplomacy.
- **Mars** — drive, assertion, action, conflict, physical energy.
- **Jupiter** — growth, meaning, luck-by-expansion, generosity, excess.
- **Saturn** — structure, discipline, limitation, time, responsibility, the
  "grown-up" lessons.
- **Uranus** — change, freedom, disruption, invention, the unexpected.
- **Neptune** — imagination, ideals, dissolution, confusion, compassion.
- **Pluto** — power, transformation, control, deep renewal, crisis-then-growth.
- **Mean Node** — the growth direction of this lifetime (traditional:
  south-node patterns to release).

Retrograde planets: the energy turns inward first — the person works the
theme internally before (or instead of) expressing it outwardly. Never
interpret retrograde as "broken."

## Signs in one line

Aries: direct, initiating, impatient. Taurus: steady, sensory, stubborn.
Gemini: curious, quick, dual. Cancer: feeling, protective, cyclical.
Leo: expressive, proud, warm. Virgo: precise, useful, critical.
Libra: relational, fair, indecisive. Scorpio: intense, transformative,
private. Sagittarius: expansive, principled, restless. Capricorn: ambitious,
structured, enduring. Aquarius: independent, inventive, detached.
Pisces: receptive, imaginative, porous.

## Aspects (how factors combine)

- **Conjunction (0°)** — fused: the two energies act as one; the most
  potent single signature.
- **Sextile (60°)** — opportunity: easy cooperation, needs a nudge to use.
- **Square (90°)** — tension: friction that produces action; the engine of
  growth when handled.
- **Trine (120°)** — ease: natural talent, flows without effort; can be
  underused.
- **Opposition (180°)** — polarity: pull between two needs; resolution
  through integration or balance.

Applying aspects = still forming, felt as "in progress." Separating = recently
exact, the theme is settling into the personality. Wider orbs = softer
influence; the script's orbs are the standard, do not stretch them.

## Interpretation craft

1. **Lead with the question.** The client asked about a career move — start
   with MC/10th/Saturn, not with their Venus.
2. **Name the placements.** "Your Sun at 24° Gemini" beats "you are a
   Gemini." Specificity is the personalization bar.
3. **Read patterns, not parts.** Sun square Moon + Venus trine Saturn means
   something together, not three separate horoscopes.
4. **Honor the mixed evidence.** If the chart supports and complicates the
   client's plan, say both. Cherry-picking is a Gate-3 failure.
5. **Speak in tendencies, not fate.** "Conditions favor a move in the late
   spring window" — never "this will definitely happen."
6. **Know what you are not doing.** No medical, legal, or financial
   prescriptions. No guaranteeing outcomes. The disclaimer is standard.

## Timing (forecast / solar-return services only)

- Transits of **Saturn** to a natal factor: maturation, delay, or a
  structure-building test (about 2.5 years per sign — its lessons run long).
- Transits of **Jupiter**: expansion windows, opportunities to grow
  (~1 year per sign).
- Transits of **Uranus**: change events, breakthroughs, upsets (7 years per
  sign; the ~90° "crisis" squares and oppositions are the shake-ups).
- Transits of **Pluto**: slow transformation of the area of life touched
  (12-30 years per sign — generational, felt only when aspecting natal
  points).
- Always date windows with the actual transit degree against the natal
  degree (e.g. "Saturn at 24° Capricorn was on your natal Sun's degree
  early this year — the reckoning you went through in February was that
  transit").
